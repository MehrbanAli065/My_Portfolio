How a build actually goes

  1. Map the real process

    The steps as they happen, not as the SOP describes them — including the exceptions people handle without noticing. Then count the volume, because that decides the architecture.

  2. Build on a queue

    One record is one transaction. A run that breaks on record 40 keeps the other 39, and the failure carries a reason instead of a stack trace.

  3. Harden before handover

    try/catch on every step that touches a system I do not control, retry scopes, screenshots on failure, and log messages written for whoever reads them at 8am.

  4. Hand it over properly

    Scheduled in Orchestrator, documented, and demonstrated to someone on the client side who can restart it without calling me.

01Healthcare

Clinic Booking & Scheduling Robots

Multi-site mental-health clinic group, Canada · Automaxion LLC · 2026

Problem

A clinic group was booking, cancelling, rescheduling and setting up recurring appointment series by hand in its practice-management system, and checking a card was on file before every session. At 100+ bookings a day that is a full-time job, and a slip is a missed appointment.

Approach

Four robots split by operation, fed by Orchestrator queue items rather than a list to walk through. Each appointment becomes one transaction that either succeeds, fails with a reason, or comes back as a retry.

Outcome

The book is kept unattended at 100+ bookings a day. A run that breaks on record 40 keeps the other 39, and 26 try/catch blocks plus 108 log messages mean a bad night is readable the next morning.

02Voice AI

Multi-Channel AI Agent Suite — Voice, SMS and Email

The layer in front of those robots · Automaxion LLC · 2026

Problem

Calls, texts and emails all landed on the same small team asking the same things — services, pricing, insurance, locations — and every one had to be answered before a booking could even begin.

Approach

Four agents, one per channel, all reading one shared knowledge base. The agent handles the conversation and ends by writing a structured outcome the booking robots pick up. Neither side is asked to do the other’s job.

Outcome

Every channel answers from the same source, so a policy change is edited once instead of in four prompts. The inbound prompt is on its 216th published revision — tuned against real calls, not written once and left.

03Retail · Data

Competitor Price & Stock Tracker — 240+ Shopify Stores, Daily

E-commerce competitor intelligence · Automaxion LLC · 2026

Problem

Watching competitor pricing across 240+ Shopify stores meant a choice between storing a full snapshot every day — about 180M rows a year — or losing the history that makes a price move mean anything.

Approach

Robots collect one export per store into Drive; ingestion writes only what changed, at variant level. The comparison runs inside PostgreSQL as a function, so the nightly workflow and the manual script cannot write different history for the same store.

Outcome

Measured on a real day: 7,558 variants produced 136 history rows — 1.8% of a snapshot, and every question a snapshot could answer is still answerable. About 5.5M rows a year instead of 180M.

  • 1.8%of a full snapshot: 7,558 variants produced 136 history rows
  • 5.5Mrows a year instead of ~180M, every question still answerable
  • 240+stores collected daily by three cooperating robots
  • 10tables, with history that is never overwritten
  • UiPath
  • n8n

  • Node.js

04Agentic AI · SaaS

AI Scoping Pipeline — Client Conversations into Delivery-Ready Tasks

Internal delivery platform · Automaxion LLC · 2026

Problem

Turning a client’s calls, documents and message threads into scoped engineering tasks was slow, read differently from one project manager to the next, and had to be redone from scratch every time scope moved.

Approach

Four AI stages — brief, epics, journeys, tasks — with a PM reviewing between each, so the next stage builds on a decision rather than on a draft. Every stage versioned append-only; the push to ClickUp goes through a mapping table.

Outcome

Re-running the sync updates the tasks it created instead of filing the plan twice, and no regeneration can destroy what a PM wrote. 23,769 lines across 137 files, deployed and in use.

  • 23,769lines across 137 files, built and deployed end to end
  • 15tables, including versioned prompt configs and captured PM edits

05Legal

Legal Case Intake Automation — Two-Phase Record Matching

US county court records · SYBROS TECH · 2025

Problem

Case intake for a US county meant looking up each inmate on the jail portal, then finding the matching case on a separate Clerk of Court site and saving the summary — by hand, one record at a time, with an image verification step in the way.

Approach

Two independent phases: one builds the roster, the other matches each record and saves the case summary with its source PDF beside it. The verification image is read with Google OCR inside a retry scope.

Outcome

Every row traces back to the document it came from. A failure while matching never sends the robot back through the jail portal, and an unreadable image costs one retry rather than the whole run.

  • 2independent phases, so a matching failure never re-runs the lookup
  • 1retry scope, wrapping the only step that genuinely fails intermittently
  • UiPath
  • Google OCR

Stack
UiPath · UI Automation · structured data extraction · Image Activities · Google OCR · retry scopes · Excel
